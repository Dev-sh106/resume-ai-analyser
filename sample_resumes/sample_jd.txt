Senior Backend Engineer - Cloud Infrastructure

About the Role:
We are looking for a Senior Backend Engineer to join our high-growth platform engineering team. You will architect, build, and maintain mission-critical distributed services, cloud infrastructure, and data pipelines.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and deploy reliable, high-performance microservices and REST APIs.
- Manage and scale cloud infrastructure on AWS using Docker, Kubernetes, and Terraform.
- Optimize relational database queries in PostgreSQL and implement caching layers with Redis.
- Champion CI/CD automation, unit testing, and agile engineering best practices.
- Collaborate with frontend engineers, product managers, and architects to deliver customer value.

Requirements & Qualifications:
- 5+ years of software development experience with Python, Go, or TypeScript.
- Strong proficiency with FastAPI, Django, or modern backend frameworks.
- Production experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and AWS cloud services (EC2, S3, Lambda).
- In-depth knowledge of PostgreSQL, Redis, and distributed systems architecture.
- Hands-on experience building automated CI/CD pipelines (e.g. GitHub Actions).
- Excellent communication and cross-functional leadership skills.
